Managing emotions can be difficult. The first step to understanding what works in the real world is to understand what people are doing in the real world. It turns out that this can be much more complex than we have realized before. people do many things...its accepted efficacy can be somewhat idosyncratic...

We are trying to get a sense of what real world regulation looks like and when it works for whom. In one study, we documented the diversity of tools people used from day to day to deal with their emotions and identified underlying features of combinations of strategies, rather than single strategies, that predicted effective regulation. In subsequent work, we are expanding on these findings to use novel ways to measure, model, and understand patterns of strategy use and efficacy in the complexity of everyday life.
